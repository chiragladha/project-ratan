## Brochure opportunity

The new studio solves the presentation layer: reference + revision, comparison options, photographs, editable prices, tax/delivery and directly downloadable PDF. It does not claim to solve authentication, live inventory or automatic quote-to-order acceptance. Private Sheet export/import is the first integration; authenticated record lookup, saved quote versions and writeback are the next technical phase.
