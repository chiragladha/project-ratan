# Ratan: Ahmedabad go-to-market plan

Research reviewed 24 September 2026. This is a proposed operating plan, not a forecast or claim of measured demand. No ads, messages or subscriptions have been purchased or launched.

## The recommendation

Start as **Ahmedabad's material sourcing desk for small interior teams**, with homeowners as a second route—not an India-wide marketplace on day one. The initial offer: “Send your interior material list. Get a clear, comparable proposal and one person coordinating the sourcing.” Brochures make decisions easier; dependable specification, availability and issue resolution make customers return.

Your existing customer relationships are a stronger first acquisition channel than an unproven broad ad campaign. Prove three things before scaling: customers reorder, orders remain profitable after delivery and follow-up effort, and the desk can handle more enquiries without slowing down.

| [EightxFour](https://www.eightbyfour.com/) emphasises one material list and one quote, and separates stocked from sourced-on-request ranges. | “One quote” alone is not unique. Ratan must make its quote clearer, follow-up more dependable and local fulfilment accountable. | Competitor SKU counts and response-time claims are self-published, not independently verified performance. |

These are positioning/product benchmarks, not a complete census of Ahmedabad dealers. Ratan also competes with the buyer's trusted local dealer, their carpenter's WhatsApp network and direct supplier relationships. Interview those buyers before deciding that technology is their primary missing piece.

## Pick a narrow first customer

1. **Repeat carpenters, contractors and small interior studios:** frequent requirements, existing relationships, specification familiarity. Offer plywood + laminate + hardware coordination. Measure repeat contribution, not just first-order sales.
2. **Homeowners already working with a carpenter:** help compare finishes and prices against a real list. Avoid attracting only free-design requests. Start with wardrobe/TV-unit material checklists, not “we design your whole home.”
3. **Bulk/project buyers:** separate qualification: quantities, delivery area, spec acceptance, billing/payment terms and required date. Do not offer credit merely to win a lead.

Lights, fans, paints and wallpapers belong in the range, but initially source them on request. Expand active promotion category by category once there are reliable alternate suppliers, acceptable margins and a complaint process. Do not advertise every category equally just because it appears on the website.

## Ahmedabad-only territory strategy

Begin with the existing store/customer catchment and actual delivery capability. Ask the owner to mark the first service area and delivery charges, then interview buyers in two small clusters. Possible field-test clusters are west/southwest Ahmedabad (Bodakdev, Satellite, Prahlad Nagar, Bopal/Shela) and the existing customer cluster wherever it is located. These are hypotheses for fieldwork—not claims about current demand density or confirmed delivery coverage. Keep Gandhinagar and distant regions out of the first campaign unless explicitly included operationally.

For every enquiry record area, acquisition source, buyer type, specification completeness and whether delivery is feasible. After 20–30 qualified conversations, decide where the strongest repeat demand actually sits. Do not create dozens of near-identical locality pages to imply branches you do not have.

## Offer design: sell a decision, not a directory

**Trade list review:** “Send your next material list. We’ll clarify specifications and compare suitable options.” No promise of always beating every dealer.

**Homeowner finish shortlist:** “Three suitable looks for your wardrobe, with codes, quantities and clearly separated costs.” Include a physical sample approval step. A swatch is not evidence of how a whole installed room looks.

**Bulk sourcing desk:** “One coordinated requirement across material categories.” Explicitly clarify partial delivery, substitutions, unloading, damage acceptance and payment terms in the quote.

The quote brochure should show: enquiry reference, revision, expiry, material brand/code/grade/thickness/size, quantity/unit, unit rate, tax, delivery/unloading assumptions, option differences, availability checked date, sample approval and the exact next action. Do not compare different specifications as if price were the only difference. Never expose supplier margin or private supplier data in a customer brochure.

## Channel plan in priority order

### 1. Existing relationships and small trade visits

Make a private list of existing customers who can appropriately be contacted. Start with 15 conversations, not a bulk broadcast. Ask to quote one upcoming list and watch how the carpenter currently chooses material. Carry actual swatches and a sample brochure with clearly labelled sample prices. Ask what would make them switch one order, not whether they “like the idea.”

Offer a small material-comparison session for local studios. The [IIID Ahmedabad chapter](https://iiid.in/ahmedabad) is a real professional-network entry point; ask about participation and appropriate trade opportunities. Its page showed no listed September 2026 events when reviewed—do not assume an upcoming event or endorsement. Do not scrape committee contact lists into marketing blasts.

### 2. Google Business Profile and search demand

Verify the legitimate business location/profile; match business name, phone, address and hours across the website and Maps. Add real shop/stock/sample photos, accurate categories and a concise description. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence; complete, accurate information and real customer reviews matter. There is no guaranteed paid route to organic local ranking. [Google guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).

After fulfilled orders, ask customers for an honest review using the profile review link. No fake reviews, no incentives conditional on positive feedback, no review gating. [Review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en).

Before scaling search spend, publish useful static category pages for laminates, plywood, hardware and interior sourcing in Ahmedabad. Each needs actual scope, images, specification guidance, contact and delivery qualification. Add business structured data only with verified facts. Do not mark generic unpriced categories as purchasable in-stock products. Domain choice is less urgent than correct business facts and a working form.

### 3. Small, measurable Google Search test

Candidate intent themes: “laminate supplier Ahmedabad”, “plywood laminate hardware supplier Ahmedabad”, “woodgrain laminate Ahmedabad”, “bulk plywood quote Ahmedabad”. Treat these as hypotheses; use Keyword Planner/account data to check actual volume and cost. No researched CPC or market-size estimate is asserted here.

Use Ahmedabad-only location targeting and **Presence: people in or regularly in the targeted locations**, not the broader presence-or-interest default. Review geographic reports and actual lead areas; targeting is not a perfect geofence. [Location options](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en), [outside-area clicks](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9376662?hl=en-GB).

Start with narrow phrase/exact-intent groups, not national broad campaigns. Review actual search terms; exclude jobs, courses, free downloads and unrelated furniture services only when the data supports it. Point laminate queries to laminate discovery and trade queries to the material-list route. Schedule call-led activity when someone can answer. Enquiry acceptance can remain available outside those hours.

### 4. WhatsApp as conversion support, not unsolicited acquisition

Use the business profile, catalogue references, saved replies and labels: New, Need specification, Quoted, Follow-up, Won/Lost. Send brochures in response to a real enquiry; include its reference and one clear question (“Which option should we arrange a sample for?”). Do not treat the website's default enquiry checkbox as permission for promotional broadcasts.

WhatsApp requires permission for subsequent contact and respect for opt-outs. Platform-initiated conversations and messages outside its customer-service window have template requirements; confirm the current rules before automating. [WhatsApp business policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy). No automated WhatsApp sending is enabled in this release.

### 5. Instagram and Gujarati content as proof

Show material comparisons, not generic luxury-home stock imagery. Useful short pieces: one finish in daylight vs warm light; how a code maps to a sample; what is included in a quote; a real bundle with taxes/delivery explained; a delivery acceptance checklist. Obtain customer/photo permissions. Gujarati voice-over with English material codes can help trade communication; have a fluent local reviewer check the wording. Test two proof-led creatives before buying broad reach.

## First 30 days: deliberately small experiment

Proposed cap **₹15,000**, subject to owner approval—not money spent or a performance forecast: ₹6,000 search, ₹3,000 real photography/content, ₹3,000 samples/trade visits, ₹3,000 reserve. If the budget is tighter, start with customer conversations and the Business Profile; do not spread ₹3,000 across five ad channels.

| Week | Deliverable | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15 existing-buyer interviews; sample/stock proof; supplier rate template; two complete test orders | Which category and buyer type produces a usable list? |
| 2 | Quote 10 genuine requirements; send comparable brochures; log every lost reason | Are customers choosing, or only collecting prices? |
| 3 | Run the capped search test only if enquiry saving and follow-up are reliable | Which keyword + area gives qualified leads? |
| 4 | Review contribution, service time, lead quality and repeat intent | Scale one segment; pause the rest. |

Suggested experiment thresholds: pause paid acquisition if 10 paid enquiries yield fewer than 3 genuinely sourceable requirements; stop/rework any offer whose delivery/handling costs erase contribution. These are management guardrails, not statistically proven benchmarks. Small samples are noisy—use call notes, not just percentages.

## Measurement that matters

For each lead: reference, source/campaign (no PII in URLs), buyer type, area, categories, qualified yes/no and reason, owner, received/contacted/quoted dates, quoted value, accepted value, supplier purchase cost, delivery/handling cost, discount, payment/collection cost, return/rework cost, outcome and next follow-up.

- Qualified lead = reachable buyer with a material requirement in a serviceable area; not merely a form submission.
- Quote-to-order rate = accepted quotes / issued quotes for a defined cohort, not all incoming leads.
- Contribution = revenue excluding tax minus purchase cost excluding recoverable tax, delivery subsidy, handling, payment costs, discounts and expected return/rework costs. Have the accountant verify treatment.
- CAC = attributable acquisition spend / new paying customers. Cost per lead alone is not a success metric.
- Repeat: customers placing another order within 30/60/90 days; keep the cohorts distinct.
- Time: median and slower-end response/quote time; quote clock begins once the brief is complete.

Illustration only: if collected order revenue is ₹25,000, purchase cost ₹21,000 and variable operating costs ₹1,500, pre-acquisition contribution is ₹2,500. Spending ₹3,000 to acquire that order loses ₹500 before overhead. A beautiful brochure does not change that arithmetic. Do not assume repeats will repay losses until measured.

## 60–90 day expansion gate

Expand a category only when there are at least two dependable sourcing options, transparent substitutions, a current rate/stock-check routine and repeat orders with positive contribution. Add more catalogues through a reviewed import pipeline: publisher/date → exact codes → finish/spec → source page → cropped preview → human approval. Image extraction is not stock confirmation. Keep supplier identities private where appropriate but show customers the actual material specifications.

At higher lead volume, add owner alerts, overdue follow-up reminders, authenticated quote storage, server-verified anti-bot controls and a durable order/quote database. Introduce real AI only after deterministic quantity rules and human review exist. Do not add credit, full checkout or unconditional delivery guarantees ahead of operating capacity.

## What the owner must supply before a serious campaign

Verified address text and service catchment; business hours and who answers; 8–12 genuine shop/material photos; permission to use catalogue imagery; accurate brand relationships; GST/billing facts if displayed; sample policy; delivery/unloading/damage terms; returns/substitution rules; sourcing margin policy; retention policy; one desk owner and backup; approval of campaign budget. None of these should be invented to make the website look established.

## Honest verdict

Yes, this can become a meaningful local sourcing business. The website is a useful intake and decision tool, not the business moat. The defendable part is a reliable network, accurate specs, remembered customer preferences, one accountable contact and profitable repeat fulfilment. Win one category/customer cluster in Ahmedabad before advertising “anything, anywhere.”
