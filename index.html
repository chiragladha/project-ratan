A few things, made clear.

Can I buy just one type of material?

Yes. Add individual materials or send an enquiry. Minimum quantities depend on the material and supplier and are confirmed in your quote.

Do you offer installation or sell online?

This prototype supports material discovery and enquiries. Installation, payments and online checkout are not included. The Ratan desk confirms the supply arrangement with you.

Can I source lights, fans, paints or digital wall prints?

Yes—include them in your requirement. Our desk will check suitable options and availability along with your boards, laminates and hardware.

Who will call me? Will my details be public?

Ratan’s sourcing desk handles your enquiry. Your contact details are for the team’s follow-up, not a public supplier directory.
